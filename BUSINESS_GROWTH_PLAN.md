# المحترفين في الطرنيب — Growth & Monetization Plan

## الهدف
تعظيم الانتشار والاحتفاظ بالمستخدمين والإيراد مع الحفاظ على جودة اللعب والنزاهة.

## Funnel
1. Store impression
2. Install
3. Guest entry
4. First match started
5. First match completed
6. Day-1 return
7. Day-7 return
8. Invite/share
9. Premium conversion
10. Revenue per active user

## Product growth
- Guest play before registration.
- Fast room creation and invite links.
- Friend/rematch flow after every completed match.
- Daily/weekly challenges.
- Seasons, ranks and leaderboards.
- Shareable results/cards.
- Cross-game profile and progression.
- On-demand game installation where technically appropriate; never fake or inflate store installs.

## Monetization
- Free core gameplay.
- Premium/VIP subscription: ad-free experience and clearly disclosed cosmetic/convenience benefits.
- Cosmetic purchases: tables, cards, avatars, effects and themes.
- Carefully placed ads in non-disruptive locations for free users.
- Optional rewarded ads only where they do not compromise competitive integrity.
- Any paid competition or prize mechanism must be reviewed for store-policy and local legal compliance before implementation.

## Measurement
Track per game and cohort:
- installs
- first game start
- completed games
- D1/D7/D30 retention
- invitations
- sessions per active user
- ad impressions/revenue
- premium conversion
- ARPDAU / ARPU
- crash-free sessions

## Guardrails
- Never fabricate, incentivize, or manipulate store-install counts.
- Never use gambling-like mechanics for paid random rewards.
- No pay-to-win in ranked games.
- No ads during critical gameplay actions.
- Privacy-safe analytics with consent/appropriate disclosures.

## Priority
1. Make Tarneeb excellent and reliable.
2. Make Mahbousa excellent and reliable.
3. Launch the game hub and growth loops.
4. Add the next games only when their QA gates pass.
5. Optimize monetization from real usage data rather than guesses.
